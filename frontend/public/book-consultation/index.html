FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Umang Superspeciality Hospital.

Where is Umang Superspeciality Hospital located?
Building No. 306, Opposite Radha Swami Satsang Bhawan, Pataudi Road, Civil Lines, Gurugram - 122001, Haryana. Landmark: Opposite Radha Swami Satsang Bhawan on Pataudi Road. Emergency entrance is clearly marked with red cross signage. Ample parking available.
What are the OPD timings at Umang Hospital?
OPD timings: Morning Session 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM and Evening Session 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Specific doctor timings vary by specialist. Emergency services are available 24×7 including Sundays and holidays. Always call +91 89297 33551 to confirm specific doctor timing before visiting.
Does Umang Hospital accept health insurance for cashless treatment?
Yes! Umang Hospital is empaneled with 15 major insurance companies, 11 TPAs, and 6 government schemes (CGHS, DGEHS, DVBETF, NDMC, FCI, CAPF) for cashless treatment. Always verify with your insurance company before admission that we are in their current cashless network.
What is the cost of CT Scan, X-Ray, and Ultrasound at Umang Hospital?
We offer Gurugram's lowest diagnostic rates: Digital X-Ray from ₹196 onwards, Ultrasound from ₹650 onwards, and CT Scan from ₹880 onwards. CT Scan is available 24×7 for emergencies. No appointment needed for walk-in X-rays during diagnostic hours.
Is 24×7 emergency and ambulance service available?
Yes, 24×7 ambulance service is available with oxygen supply, basic life support equipment, and trained paramedics. Emergency number: +91 85880 72727. For admitted patients, ambulance is free for the first 5 km, then ₹10/km beyond that. ACLS ambulance charges confirmed at time of booking.
How many beds and ICUs does Umang Hospital have?
Umang Superspeciality Hospital has 100 total beds including a 12-bed General ICU with ventilator support, 3-bed Surgical ICU (SICU), and 4-bed High Dependency Unit (HDU). There are 3 modular operation theatres with one available 24×7 for emergency surgeries.
What is the Comprehensive Health Check-Up Package?
The Comprehensive Health Check-Up package covers 28 lab tests across 72 health parameters, plus 3 specialist consultations (Internal Medicine, Physiotherapy, and Dietetics). Original value ₹8,500 — available at just ₹2,000. Includes CBC, diabetes screening, heart health (ECG + Lipid Profile), liver/kidney function tests, thyroid profile, vitamin levels, whole abdomen ultrasound, and chest X-ray. Walk-in available — no appointment needed. Come fasting 10-12 hours.
Is dialysis available at Umang Hospital?
Yes, Umang Superspeciality Hospital has 3 dialysis machines available 24×7. Advanced nephrology procedures including CHRT (Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy), SLED (Sustained Low-Efficiency Dialysis), and Plasmapheresis are also available. Call +91 89297 33551 to schedule.
Does Umang Hospital have NICU or PICU facilities?
NICU (Neonatal ICU) and PICU (Pediatric ICU) services are NOT currently available at Umang Superspeciality Hospital. Normal full-term deliveries, healthy newborn care, and pediatric OPD services are available. For high-risk pregnancies or premature baby needs, please call +91 85880 72727 for guidance to the nearest appropriate facility.
What are the visiting hours for admitted patients?
General Ward visiting hours: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM (maximum 1-2 visitors at a time). ICU/SICU/HDU visiting hours: 11:00 AM – 12:00 Noon and 4:00 PM – 5:00 PM (restricted, with 2 short visits per day). Family receives regular updates about ICU patient condition.
How do I book an appointment at Umang Hospital?
Three easy ways to book: (1) Call or WhatsApp +91 89297 33551 for in-person OPD booking. (2) Teleconsultation — share your preferred date and time and our team will call back to confirm. (3) Walk in directly during working hours (calling ahead recommended). For emergencies, come directly to our emergency department or call +91 85880 72727 anytime — 24×7.
